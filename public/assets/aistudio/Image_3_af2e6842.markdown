## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, forward-looking academic copilot interface designed for high-achieving university scholars, researchers, and academic strategists. The interface establishes absolute confidence through computational authority, clinical precision, and executive luxury. It strips away academic bureaucracy, replacing institutional dread with predictive clarity.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Skeuomorphism / Dimensional Layers** with **Aerospace Glassmorphism**. Physical surface physics define the spatial hierarchy: multi-tiered elevated white panels hover over luminous, deeply tinted spatial canvases, accented with frosted translucent HUD elements and subtle prismatic light diffusions. Tactile interactive surfaces evoke high-spec cognitive hardware, balancing computational power with intuitive clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes high contrast between deep computational blues and an expansive, clinical canvas. Color carries deliberate diagnostic weight rather than mere decoration.

- **Primary Canvas & Enclosures:** The outer background utilizes an airy, clinical `#F5F7FC`, while primary elevation surfaces deploy `#FFFFFF` with calculated ambient reflections. `#101A35` anchors critical headers, navigation substrates, and executive KPI monitors.
- **Atmospheric Depths:** `#172554` functions as secondary solid container depth for high-density AI telemetry pods and nighttime analytics modes.
- **Cognitive Energy & Accents:** `#3B82F6` (Royal Blue) guides core interactive flow and confirmed actions; `#60A5FA` (Electric Blue) renders trend lines, predictive confidence intervals, and luminous focus rings. `#14B8A6` (Teal) highlights optimization algorithms and model certainty metrics. `#8B5CF6` (Purple Accent) designates speculative projection paths and AI-generated counterfactual pathways.
- **Diagnostic Risk Semantics:** Academic vulnerability levels follow immediate telemetry standards: `#16A34A` indicates Low Risk (On Target), `#F59E0B` indicates Moderate Volatility (Caution / Critical Exam Dependency), and `#EF4444` signals Severe GPA Degradation Alert.
- **Typography Grading:** Primary data text relies on deep obsidian `#172033` for uncompromising legibility; supporting metadata, standard labels, and chart grid lines use `#64748B`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between technical instrument displays and human-centric readability.

- **Headlines & Metric Figures:** `Space Grotesk` introduces geometric precision, technical authority, and mathematical exactness. It is reserved for high-impact GPA scores, trajectory numbers, module titles, and upper-case tracking telemetry tags.
- **Narrative & Analytic Explanations:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a welcoming, humanist balance for long-form advisor notes, grade breakdown breakdowns, and scenario simulations.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures are enforced for metric visualizations, countdown timers, and predictive calculations to guarantee clean visual alignment along vertical axes.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes a mobile-first 4-column layout model with dynamic horizontal margins, expanding to a 6-column fluid structure on wider tablet viewpoints.

- **Grid Dynamics:** Mobile displays lock to a strict 16px (`1rem`) outer margin and 12px (`0.75rem`) gutter, ensuring maximum real-estate efficiency for dense predictive matrices and charts.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Component interiors and multi-tier modules adhere strictly to an 8pt vertical pacing baseline. Tightly associated telemetry labels pair to numeric metrics using `space-xs`, while module cards maintain an interior padding of `space-lg` to prevent visual friction amidst dense calculations.
- **Reflow Logic:** Analytics cards transition seamlessly from single-column vertical stacks on mobile into two-column paired matrix configurations on screens wider than 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional flat borders in favor of hyper-refined volumetric depth, soft ambient occlusion, and optical glass refraction.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Grounded at `#F5F7FC` with subtle vector-field point patterns or dynamic radial micro-gradients emanating from the bottom viewport.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Dimensional Panels):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card substrates floating on dual ambient shadows: an expansive soft dispersion (`blur 24px, offset-y 10px, color rgba(16, 26, 53, 0.05)`) combined with a sharp contact shadow (`blur 4px, offset-y 1px, color rgba(16, 26, 53, 0.03)`). Surfaces are bounded by a 1px micro-border of `#FFFFFF` with 40% inner stroke opacity.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Cockpit Cards & Overlays):** Interactive predictive cards feature physical lift on press (`offset-y 16px, blur 32px, rgba(16, 26, 53, 0.08)`), framed by a subtle 1px perimeter gradient transitioning from `#60A5FA` at 25% opacity down to transparent.
- **Layer 3 (Glassmorphic HUD & Telemetry Tiers):** Modal overlays, sticky metric shelves, and AI insight banners utilize backdrop-filter blur (`16px to 24px`) with a high-key translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` in light surfaces, or `rgba(16, 26, 53, 0.82)` for executive dark modules) and a bright 1px top specular shelf edge (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 Roundedness** (0.5rem base radius) to balance tactile ergonomics with surgical technical rigor.

- **Core Module Containers:** Standard analytics cards use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px), producing a soft, handheld feel that offsets high-density data.
- **Inner Sub-Modules & Data Pods:** Metric displays, scenario selector blocks, and input surfaces leverage `rounded-lg` (1.0rem / 16px).
- **Interactive Controls & Status Badges:** Buttons, chip indicators, and AI confidence badges utilize pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to communicate continuous actionability and dynamic state changes.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Forecast Engine Button:** Solid `#101A35` core fill, white typography (`Space Grotesk`, semi-bold), pill-shaped, interior specular rim light along the top border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Pressed state compresses along the Y-axis by 1px with reduced shadow depth.
- **Secondary Scenario Button:** Frosted glass background (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08)`), text in `#3B82F6`, border in `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.2)`.
- **Destructive / Reset Button:** Transparent base, text in `#EF4444`, hovering into a soft wash (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.08)`).

### Metric Gauges & Predictive Badges
- **Risk Assessment Chips:** Pill-shaped status nodes featuring a soft, ambient glow.
  - *Low Risk:* Fill `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.1)`, stroke `rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.25)`, text `#16A34A`. Includes an active, pulsing 6px radial status dot.
  - *Medium Volatility:* Fill `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, stroke `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`, text `#F59E0B`.
  - *High Hazard:* Fill `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, stroke `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`, text `#EF4444`.
- **GPA Projector Badge:** Deep `#101A35` glass badge housing tabular numbers rendered in `Space Grotesk`, framed by a faint iridescent rim (`#8B5CF6` to `#3B82F6`).

### Input Fields & Scenario Sliders
- **Simulation Grade Inputs:** Form fields deploy crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle inset depth (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(16, 26, 53, 0.04)`), an unobtrusive `#64748B` label floating in uppercase micro-type (`label-caps`), and a focus boundary of `#3B82F6` complemented by a 3px diffused outer halo (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`).
- **Variable GPA Sliders:** Multi-layered track in `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.15)` with an illuminated active gradient run (`#3B82F6` to `#14B8A6`). The thumb control is an elevated white sphere featuring an embedded central sapphire indicator and prominent drop shadow.

### Cards & Analytical Pods
- **Tier-1 Course Performance Card:** Primary `#FFFFFF` body featuring a split-level hierarchy: top header section carries course metadata and weightings; the bottom glass shelf houses dynamic confidence bars and future-grade requirements.
- **AI Prognosis Card:** Contained in an inverted `#101A35` dark glass shell, overlaid with high-contrast `#60A5FA` micro-trend lines, a glowing `#8B5CF6` inference indicator, and crisp white interpretive text.

### Interactive Lists & Breakdown Rows
- List records feature full-width micro-dividers with subtle opacity fades (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.12)`). Row states respond to tap gestures via instantaneous backdrop tinting (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.04)`), leading into slide-over course drilldowns.
